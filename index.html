Learn: Dos & Don'ts

When you strip all oil (harsh wash, scrub, alcohol toner), your barrier is damaged. Sebaceous glands get the signal "skin is dry!" and produce more sebum to compensate. You feel oilier → strip more → cycle repeats.

Rule: Never let your face feel "squeaky clean" or tight after washing.

#DoWhy
1Cleanse twice daily (AM + PM)Removes sebum + grime without over-stripping
2Use a gentle foaming/gel cleanserRemoves oil without barrier damage. pH 5–6.
3Moisturize every day (AM & PM)Oily skin still needs hydration. Skipping → compensatory oil.
4Sunscreen daily SPF 30+UV thickens sebum, worsens pigmentation & inflammation.
5Blot oil mid-day with blotting papersAbsorbs surface oil without disturbing your routine.
6Use only non-comedogenic productsPrevents pore-clogging → fewer blackheads & breakouts.
#DoWhy
7Exfoliate 1–2×/week with BHA (salicylic acid)Oil-soluble → penetrates inside pores, dissolves sebum + dead cells.
8Clay mask 1×/week (T-zone, 10 min)Absorbs excess sebum, deep clean.
9Change pillowcase 2×/weekPrevents bacteria/oil transfer overnight.
10Clean phone screen + brushesReduces bacteria contact with face.
11Sleep 7–8 hrsPoor sleep → cortisol ↑ → sebum ↑.
12Exercise regularly + shower afterReduces stress hormones, improves circulation.
13Manage stress (walks, breathing)Cortisol directly stimulates sebaceous glands.
14Drink 2–3 L water dailyDehydrated skin → compensatory oil.
#Don'tWhy It Backfires
1Over-cleansing (more than 2–3×/day)Strips barrier → reactive oil cycle. Gets oiler.
2Skip moisturizerDehydrated skin produces more oil. Also makes actives intolerable.
3Scrub / harsh physical exfoliantsMicro-tears → inflammation → more breakouts. Blackheads are inside the pore.
4Alcohol-based toners / astringentsTemporary matte → long-term barrier damage → more oil.
5Touch / pick your faceTransfers bacteria. Picking → scarring + spread of infection.
#Don'tInstead
6Oil-based / heavy comedogenic products (coconut oil, cocoa butter, lanolin)Light humectants: HA, glycerin. If oil: jojoba or squalane.
7Thick cream-based moisturizersGel-based, oil-free, non-comedogenic lotions.
8Stack multiple strong actives at onceOne at a time. Alternate nights.
9Fragranced face productsFragrance-free. Fragrance = #1 contact irritant.
10Sleep in makeup / sunscreenAlways double-cleanse at night.
11Use Mannlich de-tan wash on faceBody only. 2% Kojic Acid is too strong for facial skin.
12Over-exfoliate (more than 2×/week)1–2× max. More = barrier damage.

Diet: What to Eat & Avoid

High-GI food → Blood sugar spike → Insulin ↑ → IGF-1 ↑ → Sebum ↑ + Inflammation ↑ → Breakout

✅ Eat (reduces breakouts & oil)

  • Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines) — Omega-3, anti-inflammatory
  • Walnuts, chia, flaxseeds — Omega-3 + zinc
  • Pumpkin seeds, chickpeas, lentils — Zinc regulates sebum
  • Spinach, kale, carrots, sweet potato — Vitamin A, cell turnover
  • Berries (blueberry, strawberry) — Antioxidants
  • Whole grains (oats, brown rice, quinoa) — Low-GI, no insulin spike
  • Green tea — Antioxidant, anti-inflammatory
  • Turmeric, ginger — Curcumin/gingerols
  • Kefir, fermented foods — Probiotics, gut-skin axis

❌ Avoid / Limit (triggers oil & breakouts)

  • White bread, maida, pastries, white rice — High-GI → insulin spike
  • Sugary drinks, soda, candy — Same mechanism
  • Dairy (esp. skim milk, cheese, ice cream) — IGF-1 → oil glands
  • Whey protein / protein shakes — Raises insulin + IGF-1
  • Fried / fast food (fries, burger, pizza) — Saturated fat → inflammation
  • Red meat & processed sausages — Pro-inflammatory
  • Excess iodine (seafood-heavy diet) — Disrupts hormonal balance
  • Alcohol & excess caffeine — Dehydration + inflammation

De-Tan Progress

How it works

Kojic acid (in Mannlich) blocks new melanin production. It does NOT remove existing pigment. Old pigment leaves through natural cell turnover (~28 days face, slower on body).

What to expect (multi-year tan)

WeekWhat's happeningWhat you'll see
1–2Surface build-up washing off. Kojic acid starting to slow new melanin.Smoothish texture. No visible color change yet. Normal.
3–4Melanin production slowed. Old pigment still sitting there."Nothing visible." This is where people quit. Don't.
5–8First turnover cycles complete. Newest tan lifts.First photo-comparable difference.
8–12Older, deeper pigment clearing.Noticeable tone difference.
3–6 monthsDeep multi-year tan substantially reduced.Close to natural undertone.

Vitamin D

The rules

  • Only UVB makes vitamin D. UVA (tanning ray) does NOT.
  • Before 8 AM = zero UVB. Sun too low, atmosphere filters it out.
  • 10 AM – 1 PM = peak UVB window. (Winter: shift to 11 AM – 1 PM.)
  • Glass blocks UVB. Window = no vitamin D.
  • Indian skin (Fitzpatrick V): 15–30 min, 3–4×/week is enough.
